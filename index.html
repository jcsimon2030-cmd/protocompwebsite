The problem

Most apps hand everyone the same plan. You're not everyone.

Generic apps program you like last week never happened. They don't care how you slept, how you're recovering, whether a GLP-1 has flattened your appetite, or what else is part of your picture. Your coach guesses between check-ins. You plateau, under-recover, or grind through sessions your body wasn't ready for — and the months you're paying for don't add up to much.

What it actually does

One loop: train, fuel, check in, stay seen.

Train

Sessions that respect yesterday.

Your split, schedule, and training history drive what gets programmed — from a library of 950+ exercises. Slept badly, legs still wrecked, short on time? Today's session adjusts: fewer working sets, a tighter cap. The progression math stays exact, so your numbers keep moving week to week.

Fuel

Food targets that track you, not a formula.

Calories and protein move with your weight trend, your training day, and your goal. On a GLP-1 with no appetite? The protein floor holds while energy comes down, so you lose weight without losing the muscle you trained for. Targets you can actually hit, recalculated as you change.

Check in

Check-ins that actually change the plan.

Weekly check-ins, progress photos, and training trends land in one place. Training solo? The app turns them into plan adjustments and plain-language guidance you can question. Working with a coach? They see what drifted before your next call — not three weeks after.

Natural-first

The full app, no compounds required.

Training, nutrition, check-ins, and coaching are built for natural athletes first. Nothing useful is gated behind compound features — if that's never part of your life, you'll never notice it's there.

Complete from day one

Compound context

Extra intelligence when it's relevant.

If TRT, GLP-1s, or peptides are part of your picture, log them and your training and nutrition take that context into account. We don't prescribe, source, or sell compounds — and for clinical questions, the app points you to your clinician instead of pretending to be one.

Context, not commerce

Who it's for

Four kinds of serious.

Natural lifters

Progress without the spreadsheet.

Progressive programs, honest food targets, and a check-in loop that catches plateaus early. Everything a serious natural lifter needs, nothing they don't.

GLP-1 users

Lose the weight, keep the muscle.

Appetite down, weight moving fast — most apps have no idea what to do with that. ProtoComp holds your protein floor and programs training that protects lean mass on the way down.

Enhanced athletes

Software that doesn't pretend you don't exist.

Log what you're actually running and get programming and nutrition that account for it — without judgment, sourcing, or medical claims. Your decisions stay between you and your clinician.

Coaches

Scale attention, not admin.

A roster view that flags who needs you and why, drafted check-in replies you review before they send, and clients who actually log — because the app is worth opening.

Spreadsheets and DMs don't scale past a handful of clients. ProtoComp puts your whole roster on one screen: missed check-ins, off-plan weight trends, and contradictory signals surface on their own, so your time goes to the clients who need it this week.

Trust & safety

Clear about the line we don't cross.

✓ What ProtoComp is

  • A training and nutrition system that adapts to your context
  • A check-in loop that keeps you and your coach honest
  • Compound-aware when that's part of your picture — educational context, clearly graded by evidence
  • Private by default: export everything, delete everything, no selling your data, no training models on it

✗ What it will never be

  • Medical advice — clinical questions go to your clinician, and the app says so
  • A pharmacy or a marketplace — we don't prescribe, diagnose, treat, source, supply, or sell compounds
  • A telehealth provider — no consults, no scripts, no vendor referrals
  • A judgment machine — your choices stay yours

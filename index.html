The problem

Most apps hand everyone the same plan. You're not everyone.

Generic apps don't account for how you slept, how you're recovering, how you actually respond to training, or — when it's part of your picture — the rest of your protocol. They program you like last week never happened. Your coach guesses between check-ins. You under-recover, plateau, or grind through sessions your body wasn't ready for — and the months you're paying for don't compound the way they should.

What we built instead

AI coaching, a deterministic safety net, one coach dashboard.

Your training history, recovery, and readiness drive what gets programmed today. The AI writes and re-writes the plan; a deterministic engine sets the floor so sets, reps, and progression stay exact and repeatable — even offline.

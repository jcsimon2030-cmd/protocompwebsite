FAQ

What we get asked the most.

The basics

What is ProtoComp?
A peptide-aware training system. Your active compounds change your workouts, your macros, and what your coach sees on their dashboard. Not a tracker.
Who is this for?
Lifters who use peptides, performance enhancers, GLP-1s, hormones, or SARMs — and the coaches who manage them. If you're paying for compounds and your app doesn't know they exist, that's the gap we close.
Is this medical advice?
No. Educational tool only. Always confirm with your clinician before changing your protocol.

Peptides & compounds

Which compounds does ProtoComp support?
V1 covers peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, Ipamorelin, CJC-1295, growth hormone peptides, repair peptides, nootropic peptides), GLP-1 agonists, testosterone, SARMs, ancillaries (HCG, Anastrozole, Clomid, Nolvadex, Enclomiphene), fat-loss compounds (Clenbuterol, T3/T4), insulin, and Metformin.
Is the compound database accurate?
Every compound entry cites published research. Where data is missing, we say so. Sourced from PubMed, FDA labels, manufacturer documentation, and academic reviews.

The app

How is this different from PepAI / PeptIQ / SHOTLOG?
They track. We train you differently. Their AI is generic. Ours is corpus-grounded. They don't adjust workouts. We do. They don't have a coach dashboard. We do.
How do the AI and the math work together?
Two halves of one system. The deterministic engines compute your sets, macros, and PK curves — exact and repeatable. The AI reads your corpus, bloodwork, and history to coach on top of that floor and adapt it to you. Every AI claim cites a corpus entry.
Will my data be used for training AI models?
No. Your data is yours. We don't sell it. We don't train on it.

Coaches

How do coaches use ProtoComp?
The Coach Watchtower lets you monitor at-risk clients with compound-aware alerts — out-of-range protocols, lab signal contradictions, missed check-ins, weekly check-in flags. One screen, every client.
Can I invite my own clients?
Yes. Coach tier includes client invites, the AI Avatar Builder, and a private scratchpad for session notes.
What does the coach pricing look like?
Coach tiers start at Starter (10 clients) and scale to Enterprise. Pricing on the in-app subscribe page.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?
Supabase (Postgres) hosted in the US. Row-level security on every table — only you can read your own data.
Can I export everything?
Yes. Settings → Privacy & Legal → Export my data. Returns a JSON file with every row you own.
Can I delete my account?
Yes. Same Settings panel. Permanently deletes profile, cabinet, logs, photos, bloodwork, and coach relationships. Cannot be recovered.
Do you store controlled substance data?
We store what you enter. We don't share it. We cooperate with valid legal process per our privacy policy.

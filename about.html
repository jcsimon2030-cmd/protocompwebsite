About

Built by someone who needed it.

I was on a peptide protocol and a TRT protocol and three other compounds. Every fitness app I used treated me like a 22-year-old natural lifter. The training was wrong. The macros were wrong. My coach was guessing because no platform surfaced what was actually going on in my body.

I built ProtoComp because no one else was. Peptide trackers existed — but they were trackers, not training systems. They logged the dose. They didn't change the workout. They didn't adjust the macros. They didn't tell my coach when a marker drifted out of range.

ProtoComp is opinionated software. We don't think your coach should guess. We don't think your workout should ignore what you're taking. We don't think your fitness app should treat a peptide protocol and a vitamin schedule identically.

What we believe

Four positions we don't budge on.

AI AND DETERMINISTIC MATH, TOGETHER

Two halves of one system. The engines hold the math floor — TDEE, PK curves, dose ranges that can't drift. The AI works on that floor to generate your programming, set your macros, and coach against your corpus, bloodwork, and history. Every number it produces is anchored to the math and cited to the research.

CITATION OR SILENCE

Every AI claim about a compound cites a published-research entry in our corpus. No invented numbers. Where data is missing, we say so out loud instead of papering over it.

PRIVACY IS NOT OPTIONAL

You can export every byte of your data at any time. You can delete your account and we cascade-delete every row. We don't sell anything to anyone. Ever.

PEPTIDES ARE NOT VITAMINS

We adapt training and nutrition to what you're actually taking. A BPC-157 user trains differently than a Semaglutide user. The app should know that — and ours does.

Who's behind ProtoComp

A small team. Users of the product.

Built solo, then with a small team of athletes, coaches, and engineers who use the product themselves. We don't write features we wouldn't run on our own cabinets.

01 — Training

Your training bends to your readiness.

Sleep, HRV, and recovery roll into a readiness score every morning. On a rough day ProtoComp trims volume and intensity automatically — so you still train, without digging a hole you'll pay for later.

02 — Nutrition

Fuel that matches the day, not a spreadsheet.

Energy and macro targets recalculate against your goal, your training load, and your context — including GLP-1 use. Protein stays protected as a floor, so the things that matter don't get cut.

03 — Recovery

Every signal, accounted for.

Sleep, HRV, soreness, stress — the inputs that actually move the needle feed one loop instead of five disconnected apps that never talk to each other.

04 — Coach

Your coach, amplified — not replaced.

ProtoComp drafts the check-in replies and flags who needs attention, so coaches spend minutes where they used to spend hours. You review, adjust, and send.

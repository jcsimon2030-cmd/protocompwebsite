Don't do this

The kill list.

✗ Never ship

  • Streaks, badges, XP, flame emojis
  • Stock photos of muscular men in gyms
  • "Get shredded in 30 days" copy
  • Hero videos of generic fitness footage
  • Fake testimonials
  • "AI-powered" marketing language
  • Gradient backgrounds
  • "Trusted by 10,000+ athletes" — we don't have that

✓ Always ship

  • Flat colors only
  • Type-first hierarchy
  • Cyan glow for emphasis instead of drop shadow
  • Numbers in JetBrains Mono
  • Eyebrows in 11px mono caps cyan
  • Specific numbers ("+12% TDEE on T3")
  • Direct sentences
  • First-person plural voice

The coaching layer

Reads your week. Writes your day.

The coaching layer reads your training history, your check-ins, and your goals, then builds and continuously adapts your plan — programming, macros, and the daily brief. You can ask why, push back on a target, and get a plain-language answer grounded in evidence. It's guided training support, not a chatbot doing impressions of a human coach.

  • Generates and re-generates your sessions from your split, schedule, and recovery
  • Sets and adjusts macro targets to how your body is actually responding
  • Explains its reasoning and cites what it can support

Why this matters

Static templates can't tell the difference between a great week and a wrecked one. The coaching layer reads the difference and rewrites the day around it.

The floor · exact math

Math the guidance can't override.

Underneath the coaching layer sits plain, repeatable math for programming and nutrition. It does two jobs. It bounds the guidance — hard floors and safe ranges the coaching layer can shape but never break. And it's the backstop — if the adaptive layer is ever unavailable, the math still produces a sound, conservative plan on its own.

  • Mifflin–St Jeor baseline with activity multiplier and a hard protein floor
  • Deterministic set/rep/progression math, readiness-weighted
  • Runs offline — the plan never disappears because a model is down
Repeatable Bounded Offline-safe

Why this matters

Adaptive guidance needs a skeleton. The exact-math floor keeps an off day from turning into a bad recommendation — and keeps the app useful when the network isn't.

The evidence base

Every claim graded. Thin evidence flagged.

The coaching layer is grounded in a structured library of sports-science and physiology research, each entry graded by evidence quality. The coaching layer is required to ground its claims in that library — and when the evidence is limited or theoretical, it tells you instead of pretending otherwise.

What the coaching layer does — and what it doesn't

A clear line. Drawn on purpose.

✓ Does

  • Generates and adapts your workout programming
  • Sets macro targets on exact, repeatable math
  • Reads your trends and rewrites the day around them
  • Explains its reasoning and cites what it can support
  • Flags claims it can't ground — and stops itself

✗ Never does

  • Makes medical claims or diagnoses
  • Prescribes, sources, or recommends buying anything
  • Invents numbers the evidence can't support
  • Overrides the exact-math floor
  • Replaces your own judgment or your coach's

Educational and coaching tool only. Not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making significant changes to your training, nutrition, or health.
